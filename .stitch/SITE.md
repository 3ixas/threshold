# Threshold — Site Vision

## 1. What is this?
A premium, editorial cost-of-living calculator for people in their 20s–30s deciding whether they can afford to move out. Covers London, Basel, and Zurich. Static Vite + React + TypeScript app. No backend. Light mode, warm tones, serif editorial typography.

## 2. Tone
Honest and trustworthy. Feels like Monocle magazine built a financial tool. Premium without being cold. Not startup SaaS.

## 3. Cities
- London (33 boroughs, GBP, council tax, TfL zones, TV licence)
- Basel (~6–8 grouped districts, CHF, Krankenkasse, Serafe, BVB transport)
- Zurich (12 Kreise, CHF, Krankenkasse, Serafe, ZVV transport)
